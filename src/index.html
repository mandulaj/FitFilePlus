Chart

iDrag across the chart to zoom into a section — it is highlighted on the map and the stats update.
Scroll wheel zooms, shift + wheel pans, double-click resets.
Drag on the thin strip below the chart to pick a range.
Dotted vertical line = pause.
Drag the bar under the chart to change its height.

Whole activity

iStats for the section currently zoomed in the chart (or the whole activity). Buttons jump to the best / worst lap and the fastest / slowest continuous kilometre. Coloured dots follow the same bands as the map legend.
Metric details avg · min · max
⠿

Laps

iClick a lap to select it on chart and map; click again to deselect. Drag across several rows to select those laps together. Click a column title to sort (again to reverse, third time back to lap order). Gear icon: choose columns and turn colour dots on/off. Green row = fastest full lap, red edge = slowest.
⠿

Map

iTrack is coloured by the chosen metric (see legend). Hover the track for values at that point.
Drag along the track to select that section (chart and stats follow live); drag anywhere else to pan the map.
Follow: while hovering the chart, the map stays centred on that point and returns to the previous view when the mouse leaves the chart.
Fit zooms to the current selection. Drag the bar under the map to change its height.
